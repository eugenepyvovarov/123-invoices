## Snapshot

**B2G** e-invoices have been mandatory since July 2019. A **general B2B mandate is planned around 2027**. Exchange is typically Peppol / operators in the Estonian e-invoice ecosystem.

## B2G / B2B / B2C / reporting

| Channel | Status |
| --- | --- |
| B2G | Mandatory for suppliers to public bodies. |
| B2B | Planned ~2027 for established businesses. |
| B2C | Not the planned general mandate. |
