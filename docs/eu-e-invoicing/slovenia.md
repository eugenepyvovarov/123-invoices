**How to integrate:** [shared/peppol-en16931.md](shared/peppol-en16931.md) / e-SLOG via UJP.

## Snapshot

**B2G** e-invoices have been mandatory since 2015 (UJP / e-SLOG, Peppol for foreigners). The government has **proposed mandatory B2B e-invoicing and e-reporting from January 2027** for entities in the Slovenian Business Register.
