# Luxembourg

ISO: `LU` · Research date: 2026-07-24.

## Mandatory status (as of 2026-08-11)

| Obligation | Mandatory now? |
| --- | --- |
| B2G | **Yes** (since 2023-03 for remaining businesses) |
| B2B | **No** |

**How to integrate:** [shared/peppol-en16931.md](shared/peppol-en16931.md).

## Snapshot

**B2G** e-invoicing is mandatory (phased May 2022–March 2023) for national and foreign suppliers to public bodies, typically via Peppol. **No B2B mandate**.

## Caveats
