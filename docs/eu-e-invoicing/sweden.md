**How to integrate:** [shared/peppol-en16931.md](shared/peppol-en16931.md).

## Snapshot

**B2G** e-invoicing is mandatory (Act on Electronic Invoicing in Public Procurement, **2019-04-01**) using **Peppol BIS Billing 3.0** / EN 16931, including below EU thresholds. **B2B is voluntary**. In February 2026 the government appointed a special investigator (Dir. 2026:9) to review ViDA e-invoicing/e-reporting and possible domestic expansion (report expected 2027).

## Fit to our adapter strategy

Peppol B2G only if invoicing Swedish public bodies. No tax-clearance adapter. Revisit after the 2027 inquiry.

## Caveats

DIGG/Skatteverket/Bolagsverket have long *asked* for a B2B review; that is not a mandate.
