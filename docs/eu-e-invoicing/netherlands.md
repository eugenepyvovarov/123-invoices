| Obligation | Mandatory now? |

**How to integrate:** [shared/peppol-en16931.md](shared/peppol-en16931.md) (Digipoort / Peppol).

## Snapshot

**B2G** e-invoices are required for **central government** contracts from 2017 (Digipoort / Peppol). Municipalities are not uniformly mandated. **No domestic B2B mandate** yet. In 2026 the Ministry of Finance published options analysis for ViDA e-invoicing/DRR; domestic B2B is discussed around **2030**. Intra-EU ViDA e-invoicing and digital reporting start **1 July 2030**.

## B2G / B2B / B2C / reporting

| Channel | Status |
| --- | --- |
| B2G (central) | Mandatory for in-scope contracts. |
| B2G (local) | Often contractual, not a single statute. |
| B2B | Under study; possible domestic go-live ~2030. |
| ViDA DRR | Intra-EU from **2030-07-01**. |
