# Malta

ISO: `MT` · Research date: 2026-07-24.

## Mandatory status (as of 2026-08-11)

| Obligation | Mandatory now? |
| --- | --- |
| B2G receive | **Yes** (2014/55) |
| B2B / B2G issue | **No** |

**How to integrate:** none.

## Snapshot

Public authorities must **accept** EN 16931 invoices. There is **no general B2B or B2G issue mandate**. ViDA 2030 is the planning floor.
